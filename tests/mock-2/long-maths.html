Long Maths — Word Problems
1. A winter coat is priced at £48. In a sale it is reduced by 25%. What is the sale price of the coat? [2]
2. A jug of orange squash is made by mixing concentrate and water in the ratio 1 : 5. To make 3 litres (3000 ml) of squash, how much concentrate is needed? [3]
3. A pack of 4 identical pens costs £3.40. At the same price per pen, how much would 7 pens cost? [2]
4. A film begins at 19:40 and lasts 2 hours 35 minutes. At what time does the film end? [2]
5. There are 25 children in a class. 60% of them walk to school and the rest cycle. How many children cycle? [2]
6. A rectangular garden is 12 m long and 7 m wide. A new fence is to be put all the way round it. Fencing costs £6 per metre. What is the total cost of the fence? [3]
7. A cyclist rides 45 km in 3 hours at a steady speed. Riding at the same speed, how far would she travel in 5 hours? [3]
8. A rice recipe for 4 people uses 320 g of rice. Using the same recipe, how much rice is needed for 10 people? [2]
9. Shop A sells 6 eggs for £1.50. Shop B sells 10 eggs for £2.20. At the cheaper shop, how much less does one egg cost? [3]
10. Meera does her shopping, which comes to £13.60. She pays with a £20 note. How much change should she receive? [2]
11. At the cinema, adult tickets cost £8.50 and child tickets cost £5. A family buys 2 adult and 3 child tickets, plus one tub of popcorn for £4.75. What is the total cost? [3]
12. In a maths test marked out of 40, the pass mark is 80%. Sam scores 34 marks. By how many marks did Sam score above the pass mark? [3]
