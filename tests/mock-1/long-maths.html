Long Maths — Word Problems
1. There are 30 children in a class. All the girls are put into a hockey squad and all the boys into a football squad. Half the boys play in the football team and half the girls play in the hockey team. The football team has 11 players. How many girls play in the hockey team? [2]
2. A pottery shop sells mugs at £2 each, plates at £2.50 each and bowls at £2.75 each. What is the cost of a set of four of each item? [2]
3. A bureau de change gives $1.50 for every £1. A tourist wants $54 in dollars. How many pounds must she change? [2]
4. A club sells 100 raffle tickets at £2 each. The prizes cost the club £25, £15 and £5. What is the club's net profit from the raffle? [2]
5. A photocopier charges 0.2p per copy, and paper costs £4 per 500 sheets. A teacher makes 200 copies of a one-page notice. What is the total cost? [3]
6. Ferry tickets cost £5.50 for an adult and half price for a child. A car costs £20. What is the total cost for 2 adults, 3 children and 1 car? [3]
7. A savings account pays 1.5% interest each year. Mr Patel puts £5,000 into the account and leaves it for one year. How much is in the account at the end of the year? [2]
8. A pancake mixture using 150 g flour, 200 ml milk and 1 egg makes 6 pancakes. How much milk is needed to make 15 pancakes? [2]
9. A train is timetabled to leave at 09:48 and arrive at 11:15. Today it left 18 minutes late, but made up 5 minutes during the journey. At what time did it arrive? [3]
10. Grace has a £10 book token. She buys a book costing £6.75 and a bookmark costing £1.20. How much change is left on the token? [2]
11. Tom wants to buy a bicycle costing £72. He saves £4.50 every week. Starting from nothing, how many weeks will it take him to save enough? [2]
12. A rectangular pond measures 3 m by 4 m. It is surrounded by a border, exactly one paving stone wide, of square paving stones measuring 50 cm by 50 cm. How many paving stones are needed for the border? [3]
