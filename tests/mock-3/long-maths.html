Long Maths — Word Problems
1. A winter coat normally costs £80. In a sale it is reduced by 15%. What is the sale price of the coat? [2]
2. A bag of sweets is shared between Amy and Ben in the ratio 3 : 5. Ben receives 40 sweets. How many sweets does Amy receive? [2]
3. At a market, 5 apples cost £1.20. At the same price per apple, how much would 8 apples cost? [2]
4. A film starts at 19:45 and lasts 2 hours 35 minutes. At what time does the film finish? [2]
5. A restaurant bill comes to £60. A 10% service charge is then added. What is the total amount to pay? [2]
6. A rectangular garden measures 8 m by 5 m. A new fence is put all the way around it, costing £3 per metre. What is the total cost of the fence? [3]
7. A car travels 150 km in 3 hours at a steady speed. What is its average speed in kilometres per hour? [2]
8. A rice recipe for 4 people uses 300 g of rice. Using the same recipe, how much rice is needed for 10 people? [2]
9. A carton of juice is sold as a 6-pack for £2.40, or as single cartons at 45p each. How much cheaper is it to buy the 6-pack than to buy six singles? [3]
10. In a shop Leah buys a notebook for £3.45 and a pen for £2.80. She pays with a £10 note. How much change should she receive? [2]
11. On a map the scale is 1 cm to 5 km. Two towns are 7 cm apart on the map. What is the real distance between the towns? [2]
12. Cinema tickets cost £9 for an adult and £6 for a child. A family of 2 adults and 3 children book together. As members they get 25% off the whole order. How much do they pay? [3]
